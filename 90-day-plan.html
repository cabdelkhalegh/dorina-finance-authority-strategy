90-Day Action Plan — Week by Week

For: Dorina Salman Time budget: ~5–7 hours per week Rhythm to install: 3 posts/week (Mon educational, Wed tool/template, Fri story/journey) + 15 min/day engagement

How to use this file: every Monday, open it, find your week, and work the checklist. Don’t read ahead more than one week — the plan is designed to be lived, not studied. Companion files you’ll reach for: LINKEDIN_PROFILE_REWRITE.md (Weeks 1–2), CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md (Week 3 onward), OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md (Week 3 onward), FINANCE_SKILLS_CREDENTIALS_ROADMAP.md (Week 4), MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md (end of each month).

The 13 Weeks at a Glance

Phase Weeks Focus Exit Marker
1 — Foundation 1–2 Profile truth + visual identity + engagement warm-up Profile fully rebuilt, photo/banner live
2 — Launch 3–6 First posts, first 100 connections, first certificate enrolled 12 posts, 100+ connections, certificate underway
3 — Rhythm 7–10 Templates, Arabic track, DM relationships, collaborators 24 posts, 300+ connections, template circulating
4 — Checkpoint 11–13 Lead magnet, launch, 90-day review 35+ posts, lead magnet live, review done

What you need before Week 1 (all free or already yours): your LinkedIn login; a phone camera and a window with daylight for the photo; a free Canva account for the banner and lead magnet; a Google account for template spreadsheets; a notebook or simple sheet for tracking numbers weekly; and 30 minutes on Sunday evenings blocked in your calendar as your planning anchor.

Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

Week 1 — Tell the Truth About Who You Are

Theme: make your profile accurate, professional, and forward-looking. No posting yet.

  • Read SENDABLE_SUMMARY_FOR_DORINA.md, MASTER_STRATEGIC_PLAN.md, and this file (2 hrs)
  • Change your LinkedIn name to Dorina Salman — professional capitalization (5 min)
  • End the Sami Nahas Law Firm entry at its true end date; write 2–3 accomplishment lines for it using the rewrite in LINKEDIN_PROFILE_REWRITE.md (30 min)
  • Add your current role: Administrator, TiKiT — with the description from LINKEDIN_PROFILE_REWRITE.md (30 min)
  • Install your chosen new headline and the new About section (45 min)
  • Verify education (B.B.A., Arab Open University – Lebanon) and all three languages display correctly (10 min)
  • Read RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md fully — once, carefully (45 min)

Time budget: ~5 hrs KPI target: profile 100% accurate — name, current role, headline, About all updated.

Week 2 — Look the Part, Set the Stage

Theme: visual identity plus your engagement warm-up.

  • Take or select a professional profile photo: good natural light, plain background, business-casual, warm confident expression — a phone photo done carefully is enough (1 hr)
  • Create your LinkedIn banner using LOGO_AND_VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md — Deep Teal background, “Money, made clear. | المال بوضوح.” in the specified fonts; a free Canva account is sufficient (1.5 hrs)
  • Add the Skills list from LINKEDIN_PROFILE_REWRITE.md (e.g., Budgeting, Cash Flow Management, Financial Organization, Business Administration, Bookkeeping — matching what is genuinely true today) (20 min)
  • Turn on Creator Mode if available in your region; set topics to your niche terms (10 min)
  • Start the daily habit: 15 min/day — follow 10 finance/small-business voices (mix of Arabic and English) and thoughtfully comment on 2–3 posts per day. Comments = 2–3 sentences that add something, never just “Great post!” (1.75 hrs across week)
  • Read CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md sections on the 5 pillars and the first 10 post drafts (1 hr)

Time budget: ~5.5 hrs KPI target: photo + banner live; 14+ thoughtful comments made; 10 niche accounts followed.

Phase 2 — First Content, First 100 Connections, First Certificate (Weeks 3–6)

Week 3 — Publish Your First Posts

Theme: break the silence with your origin story.

  • Mon: publish Post #1 — your origin story (“Twelve years running operations in a law firm taught me one thing about money: chaos is expensive…”) — draft ready in CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md (45 min to personalize + publish)
  • Wed: publish Post #2 — a simple tool post (e.g., “The 20-minute monthly money review — 5 questions I ask every month”) (45 min)
  • Fri: publish Post #3 — journey post: “I’m publicly committing to becoming certified in bookkeeping. Here’s why, and here’s the plan.” (45 min)
  • Send 25 connection requests to targeted people using the scripts in OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md — Lebanese small-business owners, finance professionals, former colleagues, AOU alumni (1 hr)
  • Reply to every comment on your posts within 24 hours (ongoing)
  • Daily 15-min engagement continues (1.75 hrs)

Time budget: ~6 hrs KPI target: 3 posts live; 25 requests sent; 100% comment reply rate.

Week 4 — Choose Your First Certificate

Theme: commit to Rung 3 of the credibility ladder — in public.

  • Read FINANCE_SKILLS_CREDENTIALS_ROADMAP.md and choose your first certificate (a foundational bookkeeping/finance-fundamentals course with a verifiable certificate) (1 hr)
  • Enroll and schedule study blocks: 2 hrs/week minimum, fixed days (30 min)
  • Mon: educational post — Pillar 1 (e.g., “Budget vs. cash flow: the difference that confuses everyone, explained with one shop example”) (45 min)
  • Wed: tool post — share a one-page expense-category checklist (45 min)
  • Fri: journey post — announce the certificate you enrolled in and what Module 1 covers (30 min)
  • Send 25 more connection requests; personalize every one (1 hr)
  • Daily engagement, 15 min (1.75 hrs)

Time budget: ~6.5 hrs (certificate study begins next week within budget) KPI target: enrolled in certificate #1; 6 total posts; ~50 requests sent to date.

Week 5 — Build the Rhythm Muscle

Theme: consistency under normal-life conditions.

  • Mon/Wed/Fri posts per the pillar rotation in CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md — write Wednesday’s and Friday’s in one Sunday batch session (2 hrs)
  • Publish your first Arabic post this week (drafts in BILINGUAL_COPY_BANK.md / CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md) — polished MSA, natural tone (30 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • 25 connection requests; start noting who accepts and engages (45 min)
  • Daily engagement 15 min; add 1 thoughtful comment on a large account’s post where your niche view adds value (1.75 hrs)

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: 9 total posts (≥1 in Arabic); ~75 requests sent; certificate module 1 done.

Week 6 — First 100

Theme: cross 100 connections; first DM conversations.

  • Mon/Wed/Fri posts (batched Sunday, 2 hrs)
  • Send 25 connection requests to reach the ~100-connection mark (45 min)
  • Start 5 genuine DM conversations with engaged connections — no pitching, just the conversation starters in OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md (“Thanks for connecting — what’s the most annoying money-admin task in your business?”) (1 hr)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs)
  • Quick self-review: which 2 posts performed best so far? Note the topics and formats (15 min)

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: 100+ connections; 12 total posts; 5 DM conversations started.

Phase 3 — Rhythm, Templates, Engagement Depth (Weeks 7–10)

Week 7 — Your First Real Template

Theme: move from talking about tools to giving them.

  • Build a clean, bilingual Monthly Budget Template (Google Sheets or Excel): income, fixed, variable, savings rows; simple instructions tab in English and Arabic; brand colors from LOGO_AND_VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md (2 hrs)
  • Wed: post the template as a free give-away (“Comment ‘budget’ or DM me and I’ll send it — no strings”) (30 min)
  • Mon + Fri posts as usual (1 hr)
  • Fulfill every template request personally with a one-line warm DM — these are your future case-study candidates (45 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs) — trim elsewhere if the week overflows; the template post is the priority

Time budget: ~7 hrs (heavier week — the template is an investment) KPI target: template built and shared; 20+ requests/DM interactions from it; 15 total posts.

Week 8 — Month-2 Review & Double Down

Theme: first structured measurement.

  • Complete your first monthly review using MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md: connections, followers, posts, best/worst content, DM conversations (45 min)
  • Mon/Wed/Fri posts — repeat the format of your two best-performing posts with new topics (2 hrs)
  • 25 connection requests, now prioritizing people who engaged with the template (45 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs)

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: monthly review done; 200+ connections; 18 total posts.

Week 9 — Deepen the Arabic Track

Theme: serve the underserved side of your audience.

  • Publish 2 of this week’s 3 posts in Arabic (or bilingual EN/AR format) — Pillar 4 works beautifully here: practical multi-currency budgeting, cash-envelope systems for a cash economy. Practical, never political. (2.5 hrs)
  • Ask your audience a genuine question post: “What’s the money topic you wish someone explained simply — in Arabic?” and collect answers for future content (30 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • 20 connection requests toward Arabic-speaking business communities (45 min)
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs)

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: 21 total posts; a list of 10+ audience-sourced content ideas; 250+ connections.

Week 10 — Conversations into Relationships

Theme: network depth over width.

  • Mon/Wed/Fri posts (batched, 2 hrs)
  • Hold 5+ meaningful DM conversations this week; where natural, offer a free 20-minute “money clarity chat” to 2 people — practice, not sales, and within the boundaries in RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md (1.5 hrs)
  • Identify 3 potential collaborators (an accountant, a business coach, an SME community admin) and open contact with the partnership scripts in OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md (45 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs — aim to be past the halfway mark
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs) — batch posts tighter this week to stay near 7 hrs

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: 24 total posts; 2 clarity chats held; 3 collaborator contacts opened; 300+ connections.

Phase 4 — Review, Lead Magnet, 90-Day Checkpoint (Weeks 11–13)

Week 11 — Build the Lead Magnet

Theme: create your first serious free asset.

  • Choose and build your lead magnet — recommended: “The Money Clarity Starter Kit” — a short bilingual PDF guide (7–10 pages: the 20-minute monthly review, expense categories, cash-flow one-pager) bundled with your budget template (3 hrs)
  • Design it on-brand: Deep Teal #0F4C5C headings, Warm Gold #D4A937 accents, Ivory background — Canva templates per LOGO_AND_VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md (1 hr)
  • Mon/Wed/Fri posts continue — Wednesday teases the kit (“Coming next week…”) (1.5 hrs)
  • Certificate study: 1.5 hrs (reduced this week only)
  • Engagement trimmed to 10 min/day this week (1.25 hrs)

Time budget: ~7 hrs (the heaviest build week — planned as such) KPI target: lead magnet 90% complete; 27 total posts.

Week 12 — Launch the Lead Magnet

Theme: give your best work away, loudly.

  • Finalize and proofread the kit — both languages, carefully (1 hr)
  • Mon: launch post in English; Wed: launch post in Arabic; Fri: journey post — “90 days ago I had 4 connections and no posts. Here’s what happened when I started.” (2 hrs)
  • Deliver the kit to every requester with a personal one-liner; log who requests it (1 hr)
  • Ask 3 warm connections if they’d share the launch post with one person who needs it (30 min)
  • Certificate study: 2 hrs
  • Daily engagement 15 min (1.75 hrs) — batch tightly

Time budget: ~7 hrs KPI target: 30 total posts; 30+ kit requests; best-reach week so far.

Week 13 — The 90-Day Checkpoint

Theme: measure honestly, celebrate honestly, plan the next 90.

  • Complete the full 90-day review in MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md against the KPI table below (1 hr)
  • Write and publish your checkpoint post — real numbers, real lessons; transparency is itself authority content (45 min)
  • Mon/Wed posts as usual; Friday is the checkpoint post (1.5 hrs)
  • Certificate: finish it, or lock the completion date and study 2 hrs (2 hrs)
  • Re-read MASTER_STRATEGIC_PLAN.md Horizon 2 and block your calendar for months 4–6: same rhythm, plus newsletter planning (45 min)
  • Small real celebration. You earned it. (mandatory)

Time budget: ~6 hrs KPI target: checkpoint complete; Horizon 2 calendar blocked.

The 90-Day KPI Table

Metric Day-0 Baseline Day-90 Target Minimum Acceptable
Connections ~4 400–500+ 250
Followers 4 300+ 150
Posts published 0 35+ 25
Arabic / bilingual posts 0 8+ 5
Certificate #1 none completed or clearly in progress (>60%) enrolled + >30%
Meaningful DM conversations 0 5+/week in weeks 10–13 3+/week
Lead magnet none 1 published, 30+ requests 1 published
Comment reply rate on own posts — 100% within 24 hrs 90%
Free clarity chats held 0 2–4 1

“Meaningful DM conversation” = a two-way exchange about someone’s actual money-organization situation or your content — not a connection auto-thank-you.

Track these weekly in a simple sheet (five minutes every Sunday); the monthly and 90-day reviews in MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md read straight off it. Hitting every target is not required to advance — hitting the “minimum acceptable” column with a clear upward trend is.

What If I Fall Behind?

You will, at some point. That’s normal, and it’s planned for:

  1. Missed a post? Skip it — do not double-post to catch up. The rhythm matters more than the count. Rejoin at the next scheduled slot.
  2. Missed a whole week (travel, illness, work crunch)? Shift everything one week later. The plan becomes 14 weeks. Nothing breaks. Do not compress two weeks into one.
  3. Two+ weeks behind or losing motivation? Drop to minimum viable mode for 2 weeks: 1 post/week (Friday journey post — the easiest to write) + 10 min engagement 3 days/week + no certificate pressure. Minimum mode kept alive beats full mode abandoned.
  4. The certificate is the bottleneck? Protect the content rhythm first — visibility compounds daily; the certificate deadline is yours to move. Extend it by 4 weeks guilt-free, once.
  5. Numbers below “minimum acceptable” at Day 90? That’s a signal, not a failure. Run the diagnosis in MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md (usually: requests not personalized, posts too general, or engagement minutes skipped), fix the one biggest issue, and run a corrected Weeks 8–13 block before starting Horizon 2.

The only true failure mode in this plan is silence for a month and quietly giving up. Everything else is just scheduling.
