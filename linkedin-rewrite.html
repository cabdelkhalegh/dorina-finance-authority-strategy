4. About Section

Recommended About — English (paste-ready, ~210 words)

For twelve years, I was the person who kept things running. As an Executive Assistant at a law firm in Lebanon, my work was order: documents filed correctly, deadlines met, billing tracked, confidences kept. Nobody called it “financial operations” — but that’s what much of it was.

Today, as an Administrator at TiKiT, a marketing company, I see the other side: how small businesses actually manage money day to day — invoices, suppliers, cash flow, and the gap between what’s earned and what’s collected.

Along the way, I noticed something. The thing I do naturally — bringing calm and structure to complicated situations — is exactly what most people are missing with their money. Not investment tips. Not get-rich advice. Just clarity: where does the money come from, where does it go, and what should I do next?

So I’ve made financial clarity my mission. I share practical, judgment-free guidance on budgeting, cash flow, and small-business money operations — in Arabic and English, shaped by the real multi-currency life we live in Lebanon and the region.

I’m not a licensed advisor, and I’ll never pretend to be. I’m the person who helps you see clearly — so every other decision gets easier.

📌 Money, made clear. | المال بوضوح

Let’s connect if you care about calmer, clearer money decisions.

11. Connection Strategy Summary

Your ~4 connections are a clean slate — you get to build a network that is 100% aligned with the new brand. Grow it in four deliberate waves (full scripts and targeting detail in OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md):

  • Wave 1 (Weeks 1–2) — The Warm Circle (~30–50): Colleagues at TiKiT, former law-firm colleagues, Arab Open University classmates and alumni, friends and family in professional roles. Personalize every request; these people will be your first likes and commenters.
  • Wave 2 (Weeks 3–6) — The Field (~100): Accountants, bookkeepers, and finance professionals in Lebanon; finance educators and content creators in the region. You’re joining a professional community, and their engagement teaches the algorithm who you are.
  • Wave 3 (Months 2–4) — The Audience (~150–250): Young professionals and small-business owners/freelancers in Lebanon and the diaspora — the people your content serves. Connect after engaging with their posts, never cold-pitch.
  • Wave 4 (Months 4+) — The Multipliers: Community managers, event organizers, podcast hosts, business-association members, and journalists covering business in Lebanon/MENA — people who can put you in front of rooms.

Daily habit: 15 minutes — accept/send a few requests, leave 2–3 substantive comments (templates in BILINGUAL_COPY_BANK.md), reply to everything on your own posts.
