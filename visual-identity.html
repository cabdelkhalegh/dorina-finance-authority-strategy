Logo & Visual Identity Guide

For: Dorina Salman Purpose: A complete visual system — logo concepts, colors, typography, templates, and ready-to-use design prompts — so that everything you publish looks like it comes from one confident, credible source from day one.

Why identity matters here: in finance, visual consistency is a trust signal. A person whose posts, banner, and templates all match reads as organized — and organized is exactly what you sell. This guide gives you a system you can run yourself in Canva or hand to a designer without a single extra decision.

1. Identity Strategy

Personal brand first. For the next 24+ months, the brand is “Dorina Salman” — a name wordmark supported by a simple symbol. People trust people before they trust firms, especially in money matters.

Future-proofed by design. The system below is built so that the future company mark (working name: Wudooh Finance — see FUTURE_CONSULTING_COMPANY_BLUEPRINT.md) inherits everything: same palette, same typefaces, same symbol language. When the firm launches, it will feel like a natural graduation, not a rebrand.

One practical note first: your LinkedIn currently displays your name in lowercase (“dorina salman”). Update it to Dorina Salman — professional capitalization is the cheapest branding upgrade available and takes ten seconds.

2. Three Logo Concepts

Concept A — “The Clear Ledger” (DS Monogram)

Idea: a refined monogram of D and S, where the counter (inner space) of the D forms an open ledger page, and the S terminates in — or sits beside — a subtle checkmark stroke. Order, verification, done-ness.

  • Symbolism: the open ledger = transparency and clean books; the checkmark = tasks completed, numbers reconciled, calm achieved.
  • Construction notes: geometric monogram on a square grid; strokes of uniform weight (roughly the stroke weight of Inter SemiBold at display size); the checkmark angled at 45°, rendered in Warm Gold #D4A937 while the letterforms sit in Deep Teal #0F4C5C. Generous internal spacing — the mark must stay legible at 32 px.
  • Lockups: monogram above or left of “DORINA SALMAN” in Playfair Display, with an optional descriptor line “Finance Clarity” in Inter, letter-spaced. Bilingual lockup: Arabic name دورينا سلمان set in Tajawal Medium beneath or mirrored to the right of the English, equal optical size.
  • Works best for: LinkedIn avatar/favicon, document headers, watermarks on templates. The most conservative, corporate-safe of the three.

Concept B — “Wudooh Mark” (وضوح Calligraphic)

Idea: the Arabic word وضوح (clarity) drawn as a modern, simplified calligraphic mark — flowing but geometric, closer to contemporary Arabic type design than to ornate classical calligraphy — paired in a bilingual lockup with the Latin name.

  • Symbolism: the brand promise is the mark. To Arabic readers it says “clarity” before a single word of copy is read; to non-Arabic readers it is an elegant, distinctive signature that signals regional rootedness.
  • Construction notes: based on a modernized Naskh/Kufi hybrid skeleton; strokes simplified to 2 weights maximum; the final ح may extend into a long horizontal baseline stroke that underlines the Latin text, unifying the bilingual lockup. Deep Teal primary; the extending baseline stroke in Warm Gold.
  • Lockups: وضوح mark on top, “DORINA SALMAN — Money, made clear.” below; or horizontal: mark left, stacked name + tagline right. Must hold up in pure single-color for stamps/watermarks.
  • Works best for: the long game — this mark graduates directly into the Wudooh Finance company identity. Strongest emotional resonance with the core Arabic-speaking audience.

Concept C — “The Compass Line” (Minimal Line Motif)

Idea: a single continuous line that reads simultaneously as a compass needle and a rising, then steadying, graph line inside a thin circle — direction and financial calm in one gesture.

  • Symbolism: the compass = guidance and orientation (“the go-to person for money clarity”); the settling graph line = volatility resolving into stability — the emotional outcome clients want.
  • Construction notes: one unbroken 2 pt line at logo scale; the circle open (a small gap at the upper right) to avoid a closed, static feel; the needle/line tip capped with a small Warm Gold dot — the “north star” accent. Extremely reduction-friendly: survives at 16 px favicon size.
  • Lockups: symbol left of “Dorina Salman” in Playfair Display with “المال بوضوح” in Tajawal beneath; symbol alone as avatar. Bilingual lockup: because the symbol is script-neutral, AR and EN lockups mirror perfectly for RTL and LTR layouts.
  • Works best for: content-heavy use — post templates, carousel corners, chart watermarks. The most modern and minimal of the three.

Recommended Concept: B — “Wudooh Mark”, supported by C as the content symbol

Why: Concept B is the only mark that carries meaning to the primary audience and it future-proofs the company launch — the personal brand and the future firm share one visual soul. Its one weakness (complexity at tiny sizes) is solved by using Concept C’s compass-line symbol as the small-scale companion (avatar corners, favicons, chart watermarks). Commission B as the primary identity and C as the secondary symbol; hold A in reserve. If budget allows only one: B.

3. Color Palette — Full Specification

Role Name HEX RGB Usage
Primary Deep Teal #0F4C5C 15, 76, 92 Headlines, logo, banner backgrounds, primary buttons/frames
Accent Warm Gold #D4A937 212, 169, 55 Highlights, key numbers, checkmarks, dividers, the “north star” dot
Background Ivory #FAF7F0 250, 247, 240 Default background for posts, documents, templates
Text Charcoal #1F2937 31, 41, 55 Body text — never pure black; softer and more modern
Tint Light Teal #E4EFF1 228, 239, 241 Secondary backgrounds, table stripes, quote-card panels

Usage ratios (visual weight per composition): 60 / 25 / 10 / 5 - ~60% Ivory (or Light Teal) as breathing space, - ~25% Deep Teal as structure, - ~10% Charcoal as text mass, - ~5% Warm Gold as deliberate, scarce emphasis. Gold is precious — if everything is gold, nothing is.

Accessibility notes: Deep Teal on Ivory and Charcoal on Ivory both pass contrast comfortably for body text. Warm Gold on Ivory is for large display text and graphic accents only — never small body copy. On Deep Teal backgrounds, use Ivory text (not gold) for paragraphs.

4. Typography

Role Typeface Weights used Notes
EN Headings Playfair Display Bold, SemiBold (italic sparingly) Editorial elegance — signals judgment and craft, not startup-generic
EN Body Inter Regular, Medium, SemiBold Supreme screen legibility for numbers and dense text
Arabic (all) Tajawal Regular, Medium, Bold Clean modern Arabic; harmonizes with Inter’s proportions

Pairing rules: 1. Playfair Display for headlines and pull-quotes only — never for paragraphs, captions, or numbers in tables. 2. Inter for everything functional: body, labels, chart axes, buttons, small print. 3. In bilingual layouts, match Tajawal’s optical size to Inter (Tajawal typically needs ~5–10% larger point size to feel equal) and keep Arabic line-height generous (1.6+). 4. Numbers inside Arabic content: keep consistent within a document — choose Eastern Arabic (٠١٢٣) or Western (0123) numerals per piece and don’t mix. 5. Fallback stacks: Playfair Display → Georgia → serif; Inter → Segoe UI → Arial → sans-serif; Tajawal → Segoe UI Arabic → Tahoma. 6. All three are free on Google Fonts — zero licensing cost for web, documents, and Canva (all are available inside Canva’s font menu).

5. Visual Style Rules

Photography direction: - Natural light, warm tones, real environments: a tidy desk, a notebook and calculator, hands over a ledger or laptop, Beirut-toned everyday settings. - Your professional headshot: warm, approachable, direct eye contact, solid or softly blurred background compatible with the palette (see the art-direction note in Section 8). - Avoid: stock-photo clichés — falling dollar bills, piggy banks, men in suits shaking hands, glowing stock tickers.

Iconography: thin-line icons (2 px stroke feel), rounded joins, single color (Deep Teal, gold accents allowed), consistent family throughout — never mix filled and outline styles in one graphic.

Chart style for finance posts: - Ivory background, minimal gridlines (Light Teal), Charcoal axis labels in Inter. - One series = Deep Teal. The single most important data point or bar = Warm Gold. Never rainbow palettes. - Every chart: a plain-language takeaway title (“Rent is 40% of this budget — that’s the lever”), not a generic label (“Expenses by category”). - Illustrative data must be labeled مثال توضيحي / Illustrative example — per RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md.

Do / Don’t:

Do Don’t
Generous white space; one idea per graphic Cram three messages into one image
Gold for the ONE thing to notice Gold headlines + gold borders + gold icons
Consistent templates, endlessly reused New style every week
Bilingual layouts with true RTL mirroring Arabic text squeezed into a left-aligned English layout
Charcoal text on Ivory Pure black on pure white, or gold body text

6. LinkedIn Banner Direction (1584 × 396 px)

Banner Concept 1 — “The Statement” (recommended to start)

  • Background: solid Deep Teal #0F4C5C, with a very subtle (5–8% opacity) large-scale line-grid or ledger-rule texture across the right half.
  • Left third (safe from the avatar overlap, which sits bottom-left): kept calm — texture only.
  • Center-right, vertically centered: “Money, made clear.” in Playfair Display Bold, Ivory, ~72 pt equivalent; directly beneath it “المال بوضوح” in Tajawal Bold, Warm Gold, mirrored alignment; beneath both, a thin gold rule (2 px, ~160 px wide), then “Dorina Salman · Finance Clarity — Business Administration” in Inter Medium, Ivory, letter-spaced small caps feel.
  • Far right edge: the compass-line symbol (Concept C) in Ivory at 15% opacity, large, bleeding off the edge.

Banner Concept 2 — “The Horizon Split”

  • Layout: horizontal split — top 60% Ivory, bottom 40% Deep Teal, separated not by a straight line but by a single thin Charcoal graph line that rises and then settles flat (the Compass Line motif stretched across the full 1584 px), with one Warm Gold dot where it settles.
  • On the Ivory field, right of center: “Money, made clear.” in Playfair Display Bold, Deep Teal; “المال بوضوح” in Tajawal Bold, Deep Teal, beneath it.
  • On the Deep Teal field, right-aligned: “Dorina Salman — helping individuals & small businesses organize their money” in Inter Regular, Ivory, one line.
  • Feel: more editorial and airy; graduate to this once the audience knows you.

Production notes (both): export at exactly 1584 × 396 px, PNG; keep all text inside the middle 60% horizontally (mobile crops the edges); check on a phone before publishing.

7. Post Template System

Build these three once (Canva recommended), then reuse forever. Consistency compounds recognition.

Template 1 — Carousel Cover (1080 × 1080 px)

  • Ivory background; Deep Teal band across the bottom 25% containing name + compass symbol in Ivory.
  • Title zone (upper 60%): hook in Playfair Display Bold, Deep Teal, max 8 words, left-aligned EN / right-aligned AR; one key word or number in Warm Gold.
  • Top-right corner: small “swipe →” / “← اسحب” cue in Inter/Tajawal, Charcoal, 40% opacity.
  • Inner slides: same frame, Inter/Tajawal body 36 pt+ equivalent, ONE idea per slide, slide number bottom-corner in Light Teal circle. Final slide: recap + soft call-to-action + handle.

Template 2 — Quote Card (1080 × 1080 px)

  • Deep Teal background; oversized Warm Gold quotation mark (or its Arabic equivalent) top-left at 20% opacity.
  • Quote in Playfair Display SemiBold (EN) or Tajawal Bold (AR), Ivory, centered, max 20 words.
  • Bottom: thin gold rule, then “Dorina Salman · المال بوضوح” in Inter/Tajawal, Ivory, small.

Template 3 — Checklist Card (1080 × 1350 px portrait)

  • Ivory background; Light Teal header panel with title in Playfair Display, Deep Teal.
  • 4–6 checklist rows: Warm Gold checkmark icon + Inter/Tajawal Charcoal text, generous row spacing, thin Light Teal divider lines.
  • Footer band: Deep Teal strip with compass symbol + name in Ivory. This is the natural home for Pillar 5 (Tools & Templates) content.

9. File & Asset Checklist

Request/produce ALL of the following for the chosen logo. Store in cloud storage under /Brand/Logo/ with clear names.

  • SVG (master vector) — primary mark, each lockup (EN, AR, bilingual), and single-color versions
  • PNG with transparent background — each variant at 2000 px, 1000 px, and 500 px widths
  • Dark-background versions — Ivory/white renditions of every mark for use on Deep Teal
  • Favicon set — 512, 192, 48, 32, 16 px PNG + .ico (use the compass symbol)
  • LinkedIn avatar crop — 800 × 800 px, tested at thumbnail size
  • LinkedIn banner — 1584 × 396 px PNG (both concepts, use one, keep one)
  • Post templates — the 3 Canva templates (Section 7) saved as a shared brand folder, with palette + fonts set in Canva’s Brand Kit
  • Watermark version — mark at 10–15% opacity PNG for template/chart corners
  • One-page brand sheet PDF — logo, HEX codes, fonts, do/don’t — to hand any future collaborator
  • Font files/links — Google Fonts links for Playfair Display, Inter, Tajawal documented in the brand sheet

Total identity budget reality check: with Canva plus AI exploration and one freelance designer pass for vector cleanup, this entire system is achievable modestly — and every asset above is reusable for years, including by Wudooh Finance on day one.
