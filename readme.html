Welcome, Dorina — Your Personal Finance Authority Package

Money, made clear. | المال بوضوح.

Dorina, this package is a complete, consultant-grade strategy built for one purpose: to turn you into a credible, trusted voice in personal finance and financial operations — the go-to person for money clarity in Lebanon and the Arabic-speaking world. Everything in here starts from where you actually are today: a Bachelor of Business Administration, twelve years of real operational experience inside a law firm, a current administrator role at TiKiT that puts you inside a marketing company every day, and fluency in both Arabic and English. That is a genuinely strong foundation — most people who talk about money online have less.

Nothing in this package asks you to pretend. You are not going to claim to be a CFO or a licensed advisor, because you are not — and you do not need to be. The plan builds your authority honestly, rung by rung: first as an Organizer (your lived experience), then an Educator (content and templates), then a Certified Practitioner (real bookkeeping and finance certificates), and finally — if you want it in 24–36 months — a Consultant with your own firm (working name: Wudooh Finance, from وضوح, “clarity”).

One more thing before you dive in: your LinkedIn today has about 4 connections, 4 followers, and no posts. That is not a weakness — it is a clean-slate advantage. You have no old content to contradict your new positioning, no audience expecting you to be someone else. You get to build this right from day one.

Start Here: Read These 3 First

  1. SENDABLE_SUMMARY_FOR_DORINA.md — the whole plan in one warm, direct letter to you (10 minutes).
  2. MASTER_STRATEGIC_PLAN.md — the full A-to-Z strategy: where you are, where you’re going, and the three-horizon roadmap (30 minutes).
  3. 90_DAY_ACTION_PLAN.md — exactly what to do, week by week, starting this week (20 minutes).

Read those three, and you will understand 80% of the package. The rest are reference documents you will use as you execute.

How to Use This Package Over 90 Days

Days 1–7 (Foundation). Read files 2, 3, and 10. Then open LINKEDIN_PROFILE_REWRITE.md and rebuild your profile: fix the name capitalization, replace the outdated “Executive Assistant at Sami Nahas Law Firm” entry (LinkedIn still shows it as current — it isn’t), add your TiKiT administrator role, and install the new headline and About section. Use LOGO_AND_VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md for your photo and banner. Skim RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md so the boundaries are in your head from day one.

Weeks 2–6 (Launch). Follow 90_DAY_ACTION_PLAN.md week by week. Publish your first posts using the ready-made drafts in CONTENT_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md, begin your first certificate from FINANCE_SKILLS_CREDENTIALS_ROADMAP.md, and start building toward your first 100 connections with the scripts in OUTREACH_AND_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md.

Weeks 7–13 (Rhythm). Settle into the weekly rhythm: 3 posts per week (Monday educational, Wednesday tool/template, Friday story) plus 15 minutes of engagement daily. Pull hooks and Arabic copy from BILINGUAL_COPY_BANK.md whenever you need them. At the end of each month, spend 30 minutes with MEASUREMENT_DASHBOARD.md to check your numbers against targets.

Day 90 (Checkpoint). Review your KPIs, celebrate what worked, adjust what didn’t, and step into the 12-month authority build described in MASTER_STRATEGIC_PLAN.md. The consulting company stays on the shelf until the launch criteria in file 8 are genuinely met — no rushing.

A practical tip: don’t try to absorb all 14 files in one sitting. This is a working toolkit, not a book. Read the first three, act on week 1, and let the rest of the package meet you where the plan takes you.

Quick Answers Before You Start

“Do I have to quit my job for this?” No. The entire plan runs on 5–7 hours a week alongside your role at TiKiT. In fact, staying employed is part of the strategy — your admin role provides income stability and daily marketing exposure while your authority grows on the side.

“Am I claiming to be a financial advisor?” Never. The plan is built on what is already true: your BBA, your operational experience, and the certificates you will actually earn. You will always stay inside your lane — financial organization, budgeting, cash-flow clarity, bookkeeping literacy — and openly refer investment, tax, and legal questions to licensed professionals. That honesty is the brand.

“What if I miss a week?” The plan expects it. There is a full “what if I fall behind” protocol at the end of 90_DAY_ACTION_PLAN.md. Nothing breaks if life happens; the plan just shifts.

“Is Wudooh Finance happening now?” No — it is a 24–36-month option, and only if the evidence at that point says “go.” For now, everything is about you as a person: your profile, your content, your skills, your network.

“Can I change things in these documents?” Yes — this is your plan, not a contract. Headline options, post drafts, and Arabic copy are all starting points written to sound like you; adjust wording freely. The only fixed parts are the honesty rules in RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md.
