Finance Skills & Credentials Roadmap

For: Dorina Salman Purpose: A practical, honest learning plan that converts your existing strengths into recognized, provable finance capability — one certificate, one project, and one public lesson at a time. Timeframe covered: Months 1–18+, aligned with the 90-day launch → 12-month authority build → 24–36 month consulting option.

The principle behind this roadmap: You are not starting from zero. You are starting from a B.B.A., twelve years of operational rigor, and bilingual fluency. This plan does not ask you to become someone else — it asks you to make what you already do visible, certified, and teachable.

1. Skills Gap Analysis

1.1 What you already have (your asset base)

Asset Why it matters for finance authority
B.B.A., Arab Open University – Lebanon Formal grounding in business administration and management. This is a real academic credential — use it openly and proudly.
12+ years of operations & administration You have run calendars, documents, vendors, correspondence, and processes for a demanding professional environment. Financial operations is, at its core, disciplined administration applied to money.
Administrative rigor Accuracy, follow-through, documentation, confidentiality — the exact traits clients fear are missing when they hand someone their numbers.
Bilingual fluency (Arabic + English) The Arabic-speaking market is underserved in clear, practical financial education. This is a genuine competitive moat, not a footnote.
Current role at TiKiT (marketing company) Daily exposure to how a small business actually runs — invoices, payments, priorities, deadlines. Real-world context most course-takers never get.

1.2 What to build (the gap)

Skill to build Current state Target state Primary vehicle
Bookkeeping mechanics Informal / experiential Certified, systematic double-entry fluency Intuit Academy Bookkeeping + a recognized certificate
Spreadsheet fluency Working knowledge Confident financial modeling basics (formulas, pivot tables, dashboards, scenario tables) Excel/Google Sheets structured practice + CFI free resources
Financial-statement literacy Partial Can read, explain, and teach an income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement CFI free courses
Personal-finance frameworks Intuitive Named, structured frameworks (zero-based budgeting, 50/30/20, envelope/digital-envelope systems, emergency-fund laddering) Self-study + coaching-framework reading (Phase 2)
Presenting financial information Untested publicly Can turn numbers into one-page visuals, carousels, and plain-language explanations in AR + EN Portfolio projects + weekly content practice

The honest summary: none of these gaps require a new degree. Every one of them is closable in months with focused, low-cost study — and each one becomes content the moment you learn it.

2. The Learning Path — Three Phases

Note on availability and pricing: course catalogs, free tiers, and prices change frequently. Every course named below was a real, widely available offering at the time of writing, but verify current availability and cost before enrolling. If a specific course has changed, substitute the nearest equivalent — the skill is the target, not the brand name.

Phase 1 — Foundations (Months 1–3, free / low-cost)

Goal: build genuine mechanical competence while your public brand launches. Everything here is free or nearly free.

# Course / Activity Provider Approx. effort What it gives you
1 Accounting Fundamentals (free) Corporate Finance Institute (CFI) ~6–8 hrs The vocabulary and logic of debits, credits, and the accounting cycle.
2 Reading Financial Statements (free) CFI ~5–7 hrs Confidence to read and explain the three core statements — the backbone of Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 content.
3 Intuit Academy Bookkeeping (Professional Certificate) Coursera (audit free; certificate low-cost, financial aid available) ~4 courses, self-paced A structured, respected bookkeeping curriculum with a completion credential. Start it in Phase 1; finish by early Phase 2.
4 QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor training Intuit (free with a free ProAdvisor account) ~10–15 hrs Hands-on fluency in the world’s most common small-business accounting software + a passable certification exam at no cost.
5 Excel / Google Sheets for finance Free tutorials (CFI free Excel course, Google Sheets training center, YouTube structured playlists) 1–2 hrs/week ongoing SUMIFS, pivot tables, data validation, conditional formatting, simple dashboards — the tools behind every template you’ll publish.

Phase 1 exit criteria (all must be true): - CFI Accounting Fundamentals and Reading Financial Statements completed, certificates saved and posted to LinkedIn. - Intuit Academy Bookkeeping at least 50% complete. - QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor basic training underway or complete. - You can build, from a blank sheet, a monthly budget tracker with formulas — without a tutorial open. - At least 2 portfolio projects (Section 3) drafted.

Phase 2 — Certification & Frameworks (Months 4–9)

Goal: convert competence into recognized credentials and a coaching-ready toolkit.

# Milestone Detail
1 Complete a recognized bookkeeping certificate Finish the Intuit Academy Bookkeeping Professional Certificate (or an equivalent recognized program). This is the moment you may honestly write “certified bookkeeper” — see the phrasing rules in RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md.
2 Xero Advisor Certification (free) Xero’s advisor certification is free and widely recognized. Holding both QuickBooks and Xero credentials signals platform-agnostic professionalism to small businesses.
3 Personal-finance coaching frameworks study Structured self-study: budgeting methodologies (zero-based, 50/30/20, pay-yourself-first), debt-reduction methods (snowball vs. avalanche — taught as educational comparison, never prescription), emergency-fund design, and goal-based savings planning. Build a one-page “framework card” for each — these become teaching assets and carousel content.
4 Optional stretch: CFI FMVA® (Financial Modeling & Valuation Analyst) A paid, deeper program. Only pursue if (a) budget allows, (b) Phase 2 core items are done, and (c) you’re leaning toward the small-business financial-ops specialization. It is a stretch, not a requirement — your positioning does not depend on it.

Phase 2 exit criteria: - Bookkeeping certificate completed and displayed on LinkedIn (Licenses & Certifications section + a post). - Xero Advisor certified. - Framework cards written for at least 6 personal-finance frameworks, in both EN and AR. - 4+ portfolio projects (Section 3) published. - You have explained a financial statement to at least one real person (friend, colleague, small-business owner) and they understood it.

Phase 3 — Specialization & Depth (Months 10–18+)

Goal: choose a lane, deepen it, and let credentials follow demand rather than precede it.

Step 1 — Choose your specialization (a deliberate decision, made with real data):

Lane A: Small-Business Financial Operations Lane B: Personal-Finance Coaching & Education
Books cleanup, monthly close routines, cash-flow tracking, invoicing systems, owner dashboards Household budgets, money-clarity sessions, savings systems, financial-organization coaching
Feeds directly into the future consulting company’s business-facing packages Feeds the family- and freelancer-facing packages and educational products
Deeper credentials: FMVA, advanced QuickBooks/Xero, management-accounting study Deeper credentials: recognized financial-coaching or financial-education programs (verify accreditation before paying)

How to choose: by Month 10 you will have real signals — which content pillars get the most saves and DMs, which requests arrive in your inbox, which work energizes you. Choose the lane the market and your energy both point to. You can serve both audiences with content; specialize in one for paid work.

Step 2 — Optional longer-term credentials (honest framing):

  • CPA, CMA, CFA: these are multi-year, exam-intensive, and in some cases jurisdiction-specific professional licenses. They are NOT required for your positioning. Your brand is clarity, organization, and education — not audit, valuation, or portfolio management. Mention of these is included here only so the decision is conscious: if, years from now, the consulting firm needs regulated capability, the plan (see FUTURE_CONSULTING_COMPANY_BLUEPRINT.md) is to partner with licensed professionals, not to spend 3–4 years becoming one unless you genuinely want to.
  • What IS worth considering in Phase 3: an advanced bookkeeping or management-accounting diploma, a recognized financial-coach certification (research accreditation carefully — this space has both credible and low-quality programs), or Arabic-language financial-training credentials that strengthen regional authority.

3. Portfolio Projects — Proof Beats Claims

Certificates say you studied. Portfolio pieces prove you can do. Build these six, in this order. Each doubles as a lead magnet, a Featured-section item on LinkedIn, and raw material for weeks of content.

Project 1 — Personal Budget Template Pack (AR/EN)

  • Deliverable: A polished Google Sheets / Excel workbook with: monthly income & expense tracker, category budget vs. actual with automatic variance highlighting, a simple savings-goal tab, and a one-page “how to use this” guide — fully bilingual (mirrored AR and EN versions, with Arabic properly right-to-left).
  • Skills proven: spreadsheet fluency, budgeting frameworks, bilingual communication.
  • Timing: Month 1–2. This is your first flagship freebie.

Project 2 — 13-Week Small-Business Cash-Flow Tracker

  • Deliverable: A rolling 13-week cash-flow forecast template: opening balance, expected inflows, committed outflows, weekly net position, and a traffic-light indicator for weeks that dip toward danger. Includes a filled-in fictional example (clearly labeled as illustrative) and instructions.
  • Skills proven: cash-flow thinking — the single most valuable skill for small-business clients.
  • Timing: Month 2–3.

Project 3 — Sample Bookkeeping Cleanup Case Study

  • Deliverable: A before/after case study: take your own records, or a volunteer small business’s books (with written permission), fully anonymized, and document the cleanup — messy starting point, the process (categorization, reconciliation, missing-document hunt), and the clean result with what the owner could suddenly see. Present as a 2-page PDF + a LinkedIn post series.
  • Skills proven: real bookkeeping mechanics, process discipline, storytelling with numbers.
  • Timing: Month 4–6 (after Intuit coursework gives you the method). Follow the anonymization and consent rules in RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md exactly.

Project 4 — Freelancer Invoice-and-Records Starter Kit

  • Deliverable: A bilingual kit for freelancers: a professional invoice template (AR/EN), a simple income/expense log, a “what to keep and for how long” records checklist, and a one-page guide to separating personal and business money.
  • Skills proven: practical financial operations for the region’s large freelance workforce.
  • Timing: Month 5–7.

Project 5 — The “Money Clarity Audit” One-Page Framework

  • Deliverable: Your signature intellectual asset: a single beautifully designed page (brand palette — Deep Teal, Warm Gold, Ivory) that walks anyone through auditing their own money situation in 5 steps (e.g., See it → Sort it → Track it → Trim it → Steer it). AR and EN versions. This framework later becomes the entry-level service of the future consulting company.
  • Skills proven: synthesis — turning everything you’ve learned into an original, memorable system.
  • Timing: Month 6–8. Iterate it; version 3 will be far better than version 1.

Project 6 — LinkedIn Carousel Series from Each Project

  • Deliverable: For each of Projects 1–5, a 6–10 slide carousel (using the templates in LOGO_AND_VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md) that teaches the core idea and offers the asset. Five projects → five carousel series → 20+ posts of high-value content.
  • Skills proven: presenting financial information visually — the public face of your authority.
  • Timing: Rolling, as each project completes.

4. Study Rhythm — The 5-Hour Week

Sustainable beats heroic. Five focused hours per week, protected like a client meeting:

Slot Duration Activity
2 weekday evenings 1 hr each Course video + notes (courses from the current phase)
Saturday morning 2 hrs Hands-on practice: spreadsheet building, QuickBooks/Xero sandbox, portfolio project work
Sunday 1 hr Review + “teach-back”: write a short plain-language summary of the week’s biggest lesson — this becomes Monday’s post draft

Rules that make it stick: 1. Same time slots every week; put them in your calendar as recurring appointments. 2. If a week collapses, do the Sunday hour only — never skip the teach-back, because it feeds the content engine. 3. Track progress in a simple study log (course, hours, one lesson learned). Reviewing it monthly is quietly motivating and feeds the measurement dashboard. 4. Finish courses. A completed free course beats three abandoned paid ones.

5. The Certificates → Content Loop

Every credential is worth three posts minimum. This loop is how private study becomes public authority:

  1. Announce the start. “This month I’m starting [course] — here’s why, and here’s what I’ll share along the way.” (Positions you as a learner-in-public — Pillar 3: The Learning Journey.)
  2. Share the lessons. 2–4 posts during study: “The thing that surprised me about balance sheets…”, “Three Excel functions that changed how I track spending…”. Teach the single clearest idea from each module in plain Arabic or English.
  3. Share the completion. Post the certificate with a reflection: what you can now do that you couldn’t before, and what’s next. Add it to LinkedIn’s Licenses & Certifications section the same day.

This loop means your credibility ladder climbs in public — from Organizer (lived experience + B.B.A. + 12 years of operations) to Educator (content and templates) to Certified practitioner (these certificates) to, eventually, Consultant. Nobody who has watched the journey will ever question how you got there.

6. Ethical & Legal Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

Your entire brand is trust. These rules protect it. The full policy lives in RISK_ETHICS_AND_COMPLIANCE.md — read it before publishing anything; the essentials are:

  • Everything you publish is educational. You explain concepts, frameworks, and tools. You do not tell any specific person what to do with their money.
  • You do not give investment advice. No recommending stocks, funds, crypto, or any security — ever, in any language, in posts or DMs.
  • You do not give tax advice or legal advice. Questions about tax filing or legal structures get a warm referral to an accountant or lawyer.
  • You never inflate your title. You are not a CFO, not a licensed financial advisor, not a wealth manager — and you never imply it. Use only the approved phrasings for your current ladder rung (exact wording in the compliance document).
  • Disclaimers are standard equipment. Every long-form piece and every template carries the standard educational disclaimer (paste-ready EN + AR versions are in the compliance document).
  • When in doubt, refer out. The referral-triggers table in the compliance document tells you exactly when and how — referring out is not weakness; it is precisely what trustworthy professionals do.
